How You Play

Survive, Hunt & Lead

Six pillars of wolf life in the valley — roam freely, read the land and keep the pack fed, watered and rested.

🌍

Vast Open World

Sculpted hills, deep pine forest, a winding river and snow-capped mountain borders to explore.

🥩

Stalk & Hunt

Get within range of a deer or rabbit and strike — prey flees the moment it senses you.

🐺

Howl to Rally

Your howl echoes across the ridge and the pack sprints back to your side, ready to move.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Water drains as you roam — follow the radar to the river and drink before thirst bites.

⛺

Rest & Recover

Drop low in the grass to recover energy and health, then continue the patrol refreshed.

🚩

Mark Territory

Plant your scent marker on the map and watch your dominance spread across the valley.
